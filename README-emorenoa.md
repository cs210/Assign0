### [Terradot](https://terradot.earth): Mobile Ground Operations App

Terradot is an ERW (enhanced rock wethering) company that captures carbon by spreading finely ground rock on farmland. This process required intensive sample collectiona nd tracking in order to demonstarte that Carbon is being captued. They are a Series A startup based in SF and Sao Paulo, with over 100 employees.

As a CS+Social Good Fellow in 2024, I designed and built their mobile Groud Opterations app. To date this app has become indispensable for the day-to-day operations of the company, allowing the ground team to collect over 100,000 samples using the app in the first year.

It was built on React Native, had a fully custom-build offline synchronization engine that run in the background and resolved conflicts automatically, and inetracted with the comapny's API,

In 2025 I returned to build a new section for the app, allowing the team to start tracking carbon emissions from their spreading and sampling operations. I also led a security improvement project fortyfing the company's databases by implementing an IDentity-Aware-Proxy allowing for Rule-Based Accesss Control to database and sesnitive systems instead of everyone just having blanket access to the systems.
