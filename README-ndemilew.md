# Hi! I’m Nardos Demilew

I’m a senior studying Computer Science and the thing that drives me most is the ability to build and create for impact. As an Ethiopian American, I’m also big on **community building** and working to solve problems that help bridge gaps between technology and people.

## What I’m into right now

- **AI for security**: how we evaluate, stress-test, and _actually measure_ what agentic systems can do in real world systems. In fact, this is the basis of my research here on campus. I work at the Stanford AI Lab (SAIL) in a cross lab collaboration with Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) to evaluate cybersecurity capabilities of AI agents.
- **Data and Bias**: I've always had a deep interest in bias in technology and particularly in Artificial Intelligence. In my research, I'm often grappling with question of introducing bias when answering an open-ended questions. I'm also on the information track for my CS degree because I find these questions of how we acquire, leverage, and aggregate data to be core to the integrity of software systems built.
- **AI integration processes**: In my experience of working at both large corporations and a startup, the ways in which people at various levels of expertise choose (or avoid) AI integration into various parts of the organization is essential to their growth and success. I've worked on prototypes or sketched out ideas on how to approach these problems for the past few years and I believe it is an essential question that business and organizations need to address.
