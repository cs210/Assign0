## Selected projects & experience

### Consumer Reports (Stanford Tech Ethics and Policy Fellowship)
I worked on a project looking at how companies handle “Do Not Sell/Share” requests in practice. I ran a mix of technical checks and user-experience walkthroughs across 400+ companies, then organized the common failure modes using a “Worst Practices” taxonomy that’s being used for an upcoming public report. Alongside the auditing work, I built a prototype LLM-powered compliance analyzer (GPT + LangChain + embeddings) using a RAG pipeline to automatically flag key features and support regulatory verification. I also wrote a couple of deeper case studies—focused on people-search and fintech—highlighting how dual data streams show up in real products and outlining practical mitigation recommendations.

### Emerging Markets Working Group — Hoover Institution
I work on research that tries to make it easier for private capital to move into emerging-market infrastructure in a way that’s actually investable. A big part of my focus is quantifying uncertainty, especially how regulatory changes can shock expected returns, and building analytical heuristics for Global South energy and telecom investments. In parallel, I’ve been digging into the geopolitical layer, looking at how Belt-and-Road dynamics show up in bilateral trade relationships and how those patterns correlate with measures like the EIU Democracy Index and G77 membership.

### Beem Africa (Stanford GSB Seed Internship Program)
I lived in Dar-es-Salaam, Tanzania for a summer to help ship a knowledge-grounded conversational AI experience on the Moja platform. I built a custom RAG pipeline using ElasticSearch, FastAPI, and GPT-4 over 10K+ enterprise documents across 40+ client deployments (primarily medical and financial data for local East African hospital and bank clients). I also ran a few internal workshops to teach LLM architectures and practical prompt engineering patterns.

### CellarEye
I built data processing pipelines for 10K+ wine label images. Short passion project!
